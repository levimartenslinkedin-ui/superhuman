FAQ

Why build a personal brand?

Putting out content generates leads without you talking to them. It exposes you to prospects across the country. It got me out of my warm market and talking to 8-figure business owners within 6 months. Plus, you can prospect in those meetings like you would in any other meeting. On top of that, an authentic personal brand builds trust before you ever talk with a prospect, either in person or virtually.

Are results guaranteed?

No one can guarantee specific numbers, but this has worked for me and for my clients. If you put in the effort and follow the strategies we talk about, LinkedIn will give you a generous return.

How does this work with compliance?

Each company is different, but advisors at Northwestern Mutual, New York Life, Edward Jones, and more have built incredibly successful practices from LinkedIn. Consult with your local compliance department for specific questions.

How long should I spend on LinkedIn each day?

The more you give, the more you get. At a minimum, 45 minutes. One of my favorite parts of building on LinkedIn as an advisor was that when meetings with prospects canceled, I could go to LinkedIn and generate 3 more leads in 15 minutes. Whether I was at the office or in line at Chipotle, I was building traction for my business.
